# Aranea Notes Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Aranea Notes (formerly GoodNatt) is a personal notes app for iPad. It has no server of its own and collects no analytics.

## Data stored on your device
Notebooks, handwriting, recordings, study plans and settings are stored only on your iPad (and in iCloud if you turn on Apple's backup).

## Google Drive backup
If you connect a Google account, Aranea uploads copies of your notebooks to a folder in **your own** Google Drive using the `drive.file` scope. The app can only see files it created itself; it cannot read other files in your Drive. Your Google sign-in is kept in the device Keychain and is used solely to upload and download these backup files. Nothing is sent anywhere else. Disconnecting the account in the app revokes the sign-in; you can also remove access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the backup folder from Drive at any time.

## Other network use
The app fetches optional template and notice listings from public GitHub pages. These requests carry no personal data.
